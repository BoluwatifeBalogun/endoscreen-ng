What it does

A screening tool that fits how a health centre really works

No specialist, no assay machine and no machine learning knowledge needed. Enter what you can observe and measure; the system does the rest.

How it works

From first contact to referral decision in three steps

  1. 01

    Enter what you have

    Age, weight, height, blood pressure, the symptoms the patient reports and any lab result that is available.

  2. 02

    The model scores the pattern

    Inputs are encoded and scaled exactly as in training, then classified across five disorders and a no-imbalance class.

  3. 03

    Act on a clear result

    A low, moderate or high risk band, the most likely condition, the reasons behind it and recommended next steps.

Conditions screened

Five common hormonal disorders in a single form

Most tools look at one disorder at a time. Patients do not present that way: fatigue and weight change could be thyroid, adrenal or blood sugar. EndoScreen NG weighs them together.
